# 🏇 Seere — Contract Verbs

---

## DISPATCH
*Route a job across machines, containers, or services.*

- "Seere, dispatch a Python script to process all JPGs in ~/Downloads and rename them by date."
- "Seere, dispatch a backup of my OpenJarvis config to ~/Galvani."
- "Seere, dispatch this shell command on a schedule every morning at 8am."

---

## CARRY
*Move data from one place to another with verification.*

- "Seere, carry my Lightroom exports from ~/Desktop to MediaVault1/Exports."
- "Seere, carry the SEERE_CONTRACT.md into the frontend public folder."
- "Seere, carry everything in ~/Downloads/to-archive to Galvani and confirm checksums."

---

## RECARRY
*Re-sync or re-deliver something that was already moved.*

- "Seere, recarry the last dataset you moved — the destination had an error."
- "Seere, recarry my project templates to the new ~/Projects location."
- "Seere, recarry the Tanas media folder — the copy on Galvani looks incomplete."

---

## REVEAL
*Surface the current state or location of something.*

- "Seere, reveal what's inside ~/OpenJarvis/frontend/src."
- "Seere, reveal which Ollama models are currently loaded."
- "Seere, reveal the disk usage breakdown on Galvani."
- "Seere, reveal all Python files modified in the last 24 hours."

---

## AUDIT
*Return the history of what happened to something.*

- "Seere, audit what changed in prompt_registry.py since the last commit."
- "Seere, audit the git log for this repo and summarize the last 10 commits."
- "Seere, audit which files were moved during the March cleanup session."

---

## DISCOVER
*Scan for lost, underused, or unknown assets.*

- "Seere, discover any duplicate files in ~/Creative Cloud Files."
- "Seere, discover unused node_modules folders across ~/Projects."
- "Seere, discover what's taking the most space inside DocumentsHHD."
- "Seere, discover any orphaned Lightroom catalogs on this machine."

---

*Verbs can be used naturally — Seere will infer intent from context.*
*You don't have to say the verb explicitly. "What's in this folder?" maps to REVEAL.*
